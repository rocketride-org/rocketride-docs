This release focuses on enhancing user adoption by streamlining installs with smaller installer footprints, improving the user experience on the Browse page, and simplifying upgrades. It also boosts the performance and accuracy of Semantic Search, and introduces iterative scanning of SharePoint Online site collections. These updates make APARAVI an even more effective tool for managing data estates, enhancing connectivity, productivity, and overall user satisfaction.

 
### Windows All-in-one installer and Simplified User Registration and Account Activation

We are excited to announce a significant update in this release that streamlines the APARAVI installation process, making it faster and easier than ever for users to get started. Our focus is on enhancing user adoption by minimizing the number of steps required to install and activate the platform, creating a smooth self-service experience.

The new installer is now available for easy download directly from our website. Once downloaded, users can quickly register, and in the background, their local platform is automatically activated. This means that without any complex setup, users can immediately begin connecting their data sources, gaining full visibility into their data estate with just a few simple steps. By simplifying the onboarding process, we are empowering users to take control of their data faster and more efficiently.

 
### SharePoint Online - Extension to recursively scan Site Collections

This release enhances the functionality of SharePoint Online to prospects or customers who have large SharePoint volume of data, not just within their root site but also within their nested site collections

What is a site collection?

A site collection is **made up of one top-level site and all associated sites below it.**

 
Now, APARAVI is capable of recursively fetching all kinds of unstructured data from these site collections and provide data intelligence to it.

SharePoint being the popular content management tool and large organizations find it quite easy to use this tool because of streamlined access, centralized management, and easier content governance. So now , APARAVI will add additional benefits to organizations by providing them data intelligence on their site collections.
