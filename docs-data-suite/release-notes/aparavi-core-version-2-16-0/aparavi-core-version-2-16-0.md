This release is designed to enhance performance, connectivity, and productivity, making APARAVI an integral tool for managing data estates effectively.

 
### **Connect to Google Drive**

This marks our first integration with Google, a significant step in expanding our offerings within the Google suite. With this new connectivity, we now support Google Drive. Administrators can scan the Google Drives of all enterprise users within their organization, utilizing APARAVI’s feature set to help prepare and manage their data for various projects.

### **Spoofed Files**

Spoofed Files is a critical feature that enhances our customers' awareness of potential risks in their data ecosystem. This feature identifies files that may be malicious by flagging discrepancies between a file's extension and its actual content. Such mismatches can indicate that a file has been tampered with or disguised, potentially posing security threats. By detecting these inconsistencies, Spoofed Files provides an early warning system, allowing customers to take proactive measures to safeguard their data and ensure the integrity of their digital environment.


### **GPU Enablement for High-Compute Tasks:**

This feature leverages the power of GPUs to perform computing tasks with unparalleled ease and efficiency. This enhancement allows users to run demanding operations seamlessly and flexibly using GPUs, covering a broad range of tasks from scans to data actions.

- **Enhanced Performance and Speed**

The integration of GPU acceleration significantly boosts the performance and speed of high compute tasks. GPUs, with their parallel processing capabilities, can handle multiple operations simultaneously, resulting in faster execution times compared to traditional CPU-based processing. This improvement is crucial for tasks like OCR and Vectorizations that require substantial computational resources, enabling businesses to achieve quicker results , maintaining accuracy and increased productivity.

- **Scalability and Flexibility**

This feature provides the flexibility to scale operations as needed. Whether the tasks involve large-scale data scans or complex data actions, GPUs can efficiently manage the load, ensuring smooth and consistent performance.

- **Cost-Effectiveness**

Utilizing GPUs for high compute tasks can lead to cost savings by optimizing resource usage. GPUs can process tasks faster and more efficiently, reducing the time and energy required for computations. This efficiency can translate to lower operational costs, making it a cost-effective solution for businesses with extensive computational needs.
