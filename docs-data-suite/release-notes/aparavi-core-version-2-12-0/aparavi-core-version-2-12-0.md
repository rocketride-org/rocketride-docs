Introducing this release brings substantial business benefits to the Legal Services and IT industry sectors. The go-to-market strategy for Aparavi in collaboration with Relativity provides a distinct advantage for professionals in Legal services. By integrating Aparavi's data intelligence capabilities with Relativity, individuals in the legal field can optimize their workflow, leveraging the power of data intelligence to enhance productivity. This integration not only saves valuable time for experts but also alleviates the challenges associated with dealing with irrelevant data, contributing to a more efficient and stress-free work environment.

Aparavi for Legal Services enriching Relativity Server 2022:

This Integration together with Relativity has several benefits in the Legal industry:

- Streamlined Data Focus:
    - Aparavi ensures a more focused approach to handling data, allowing legal professionals to concentrate on essential tasks by eliminating irrelevant information from extensive datasets.
- Metadata and Classification Integration:
    - Seamless integration with Relativity enriches the platform with valuable metadata, classification hits, and context fields, facilitating quick identification of sensitive data such as Personally Identifiable Information (PII) within documents.
- Efficient Document Review in Relativity:
    - Relativity highlights classification hits and contexts, significantly accelerating the manual review process. This not only reduces errors but also provides users with compelling evidence embedded within their documents.
- Queryable Classifications for E-Discovery:
    - Aparavi delivers both pre-built and customized classifications to Relativity, enhancing its e-discovery capabilities. Users can now extract valuable insights efficiently during the e-discovery process using these queryable classifications.

 
### Unlocking New Possibilities to Integrate using our new data action “Transform”

- This action not only expands integration possibilities but also unlocks new avenues for users, allowing them to harness Aparavi's intelligence in innovative ways within their preferred services and applications.
- This release empowers Aparavi with increased flexibility, enabling seamless integration of our intelligence into various services, business applications, and databases.

 
### Stored Data Actions

The Data Actions feature has a significant enhancement. The current automated framework is highly efficient—set your AQL query and run it on a schedule. This is perfect for those who have a clear plan and are prepared to execute.

However, what if you prefer to draft your actions first and seek approval from someone else?

With Stored Data Actions, we've transformed the automated framework into a more flexible and user-friendly experience. Now, you can create numerous actions, easily share them with others, and conveniently search through your previous drafts. The key advantage? You have the power to execute them precisely when you're ready.

 
### Data Owners and Permissions from various sources

In this release, file data owners and permissions from Azure Blob, AWS S3 and S3 Compat are gathered. Aparavi gathers file ownership and permissions, irrespective of their location. This information is gathered from all available sources, establishing a robust foundation for maintaining data integrity and security across enterprises. The gathered data on ownership and permissions aligns with our strategic vision, ensuring that users, with diverse identities, could perform any functionality within Aparavi like Data Actions, conducting file Discovery, engaging in Semantic searches, and leveraging Question and Answer capabilities—all within their designated user level access.
